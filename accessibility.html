Accessibility Statement

Micro Medical Devices · HR Recruiting Dashboard · Last updated: 6 November 2026

We want every member of our HR team to be able to use the recruiting dashboard, including people with vision, hearing, motor or cognitive disabilities.

Standard

The dashboard is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA and Israeli Standard IS 5568.

What we did

  • Works with a keyboard only: Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate, Esc to close. A "Skip to main content" link is the first item on the page.
  • Works with screen readers (NVDA on Windows, VoiceOver on iPhone). New CVs are announced automatically.
  • Status is shown with color, text and an icon together — never color alone.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5 : 1 on a white background.
  • Big icons and buttons of at least 56 × 56 pixels.
  • Works when zoomed to 200% and on phones, in portrait and landscape.
  • Animations stop when "reduce motion" is turned on in your device settings.
  • Hebrew right-to-left layout with the "עברית" button.
  • The chart can also be shown as a table.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: / moves to search, N opens notifications.

Known limitations

  • Uploaded Word files cannot be previewed in the browser; they can be downloaded.
  • The CV preview is shown in English only.

Contact

If something does not work for you, please tell us and we will help. Accessibility coordinator, HR department: accessibility@example.com

הצהרת נגישות (בקצרה)

לוח הבקרה לגיוס עובדים מותאם לתקן הישראלי ת״י 5568 ולהנחיות WCAG 2.2 ברמה AA: שימוש במקלדת בלבד, תמיכה בקוראי מסך, ניגודיות צבעים, כפתורים גדולים, הגדלה עד 200%, ותצוגה מימין לשמאל.

נתקלתם בבעיה? פנו לרכז/ת הנגישות במחלקת משאבי אנוש: accessibility@example.com
